Magic: Werewolf Pack Leader Oracle change and new rules with Star Trek

With Magic: The Gathering | Star Trek, released 13 November 2026, Werewolf Pack Leader's last ability now makes it a Werewolf until end of turn, instead of making it stop being a Human. New rules 700.17, 701.72 and 702.196 cover face a dilemma, assimilate and beam me up, and new rule 508.4e supports Kira Nerys, Firebrand letting creatures enter tapped and attacking.

Announced
6 November 2026
Takes effect
13 November 2026

What changed

Oracle change

  • Werewolf Pack Leader: its {3}{G} ability now ends with "and is a Werewolf" instead of "and isn't a Human"

Comprehensive Rules

  • 700.17: face a dilemma
  • 701.72: assimilate
  • 702.196: beam me up
  • 508.4e (new): creatures entering tapped and attacking through a replacement effect, for Kira Nerys, Firebrand
  • 208.3 (updated): power and toughness of noncreature objects that aren't on the battlefield

Not sure how this affects you? Bring your deck into the shop and we'll go through it with you. See this month's events at Real Rune Cards in Blackpool, or start with our learn-to-play guides.
