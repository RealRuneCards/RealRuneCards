Yu-Gi-Oh!: 43 Forbidden & Limited List changes from 19 October 2026

From 19 October 2026 in the Advanced Format. Forbidden: Amorphage Goliath; Amorphage Sloth; Elfnote Tinia (was limited); Magicians' Souls; Pharaonic Advent; Spell Canceller and 28 more. Limited: Droll & Lock Bird (was semi-limited); Griffoh; Perfectron Hydradrive Dragon; Superdreadnought Rail Cannon Gustav Max; Branded in High Spirits (was semi-limited); Branded Opening and 2 more. Semi-Limited: Sky Striker Mobilize - Engage!. The change to Perfectron Hydradrive Dragon applies from the release of Magnificent Maestros on 11 November 2026.
