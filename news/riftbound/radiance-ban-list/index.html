Riftbound: 8 cards and 2 battlefields banned from 23 November

Riot has added eight cards and two battlefields to the Riftbound ban list. From Monday 23 November 2026 you can't use them in Standard or 2v2 Constructed.

Format
Standard and 2v2 Constructed
Announced
13 November 2026
Takes effect
23 November 2026
Region
Worldwide. Korea and Traditional Chinese regions follow later (see below).

What changed

These eight cards are banned:

  • Riven, Broken Blade
  • Zhonya's Hourglass
  • Stellacorn Herder
  • Nocturne, Horrifying
  • Rhasa the Sunderer
  • Brynhir Thundersong
  • Dazzling Aurora
  • Baited Hook

These two battlefields are banned:

  • Emperor's Dais
  • Star Spring

No cards were unbanned on the main list, and there are no extra 2v2-only bans this time. Riot says it is looking at 2v2 and plans to share more early next year.

Who's affected

Anyone playing Standard or 2v2 Constructed at official events from 23 November. If your deck or battlefields use any of the above, you'll need replacements before then. Until 22 November, the current list still applies.

New to Riftbound? If you're playing casually with friends, you can keep using these cards. Check your deck list against the names above before an official event, or bring it in and we'll check it with you.

Regional timing

The bans won't apply to competitive events in Korea and Traditional Chinese regions until the Radiance set is released there. From 23 November, Riot is also moving from one worldwide ban list to separate lists for regions whose card pool is behind the rest of the world.

What's changing next year

From 2027, Riot will announce ban list changes at fixed times, called "ban windows": on the Friday two weeks before each Continental event, and the Friday two weeks after. Riot also says it will be a bit stricter about cards that make the game less healthy.
