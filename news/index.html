    How we write these

    • Every update links straight to the publisher's own announcement. Hand-written articles are checked against that announcement before they go up.
    • Short automatic updates are added every few hours from official publisher pages. They state only facts taken from the announcement (who announced it, the headline, dates, formats and venue when given) in our own words, and copy no publisher text or images.
    • Announced means it's official but not in force yet. Now in effect or Released means it applies today. Teased means hinted at, not confirmed.
    • Spotted a mistake? Tell us on Discord or Facebook and we'll fix it and note the change.
