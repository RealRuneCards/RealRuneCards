Who it's for

For parents who'd rather join in than switch off

Card games can be just another hobby you pay for and watch from the sidelines. Or they can be the thing you and your child do together, and learn from.

The Skill Tree is for you if…

  • Your child is into card games like Pokémon, Lorcana, Magic or Riftbound, and you don't really get it yet
  • You want more time together that isn't another screen
  • You're unsure about the money side: spending, trading, card shows and what things are worth
  • You want to handle the hard moments: losing, arguments over rules, wanting everything
  • Your child is roughly 6 to 12, though plenty applies to teens too

It isn't…

  • A guide to winning tournaments. It's about your relationship and their life skills, not deck tech.
  • A sales pitch for more cards. Whole modules cover budgeting, value and knowing when to say no.
  • Therapy. It's practical parenting guidance. If you're worried about your child's wellbeing, speak to your GP.

Questions

Before you join

I don't play card games. Is this still for me?

Yes. The course follows a family who start with no card game experience at all. You'll learn enough of the hobby to join in, and the parenting skills work whatever game your child plays.

Which games does it cover?

The ideas work with any trading card game, including Pokémon, Disney Lorcana, Magic: The Gathering, Riftbound and Yu-Gi-Oh!. Examples draw on the games families play most.

What age is it for?

It's written with 6 to 12-year-olds in mind, the age most children get into card games. Much of it, especially the money and online modules, applies to teenagers too.

How much time does it take?

Each module is designed to fit into an evening, and then into the games you're already playing. There's no deadline, so go at your own pace.

What's Skool?

Skool is the website where the course and our parent community live. It works in any web browser and has a free phone app. You'll create a free account when you join.

Is there a free way to start?

Yes, two. Module 1 is free on Skool, and our free 7 Days on the Same Side course gives you seven quick skills you can use tonight.
