Privacy Policy

We collect as little as we can, use it only for what we've told you, and never sell it.

Last updated: 28 October 2026

The short version
  • If you sign up for emails, we keep your first name and email address to send you what you asked for.
  • Our website stats don't use cookies and don't identify you.
  • Your course progress and calendar reminders stay on your own device. We never see them.
  • You can unsubscribe, or ask us to delete your details, at any time.

Who we are

This policy covers realrunecards.com, our email lists and our community channels. Real Rune Cards Ltd is a company registered in England and Wales (company number 16969346), and Level Up Together is one of its trading names. Our registered address is 74 Coronation Street, Blackpool, FY1 4PD.

We are the "controller" of the personal information described here, which means we decide how it's used and we're responsible for looking after it. If you have any questions, email us at RealRuneCards@outlook.com.

Things that stay on your device

Some features work entirely in your own browser, and nothing about them is sent to us:

  • Course progress: the days you tick off on 7 Days on the Same Side are saved in your browser only. Clearing your browser data removes them.
  • Calendar reminders: the reminder file is created on your device and goes straight into your own calendar.
  • Printing: printed course sheets are made by your own browser.

Who helps us

We use a small number of trusted services to run these features. They only use your information on our instructions:

  • Sender stores our email list and sends our emails.
  • Umami counts website visits and clicks without cookies.
  • Tally runs our feedback forms.
  • Skool hosts our online community and The Skill Tree, under its own privacy policy for members.

Some of these providers may store data outside the UK. Where they do, we only use providers that protect it with safeguards recognised under UK data protection law.

If you contact us on Discord or Facebook, those platforms handle your messages under their own privacy policies.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law, you have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the information we hold about you
  • ask us to correct anything that's wrong
  • ask us to delete your information
  • object to us using it, or ask us to limit how we use it
  • withdraw your consent at any time, for example by using the unsubscribe link in any of our emails

To use any of these rights, email RealRuneCards@outlook.com. We'll reply within one month, and it's free.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, please tell us first so we can put it right. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.

Children

Our email sign-ups are for adults. If you're under 18 and would like our beginner tips, please ask a parent or carer to sign up instead.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we use your information, we'll update this page and the date at the top. If the change is significant, we'll email subscribers before it takes effect.
