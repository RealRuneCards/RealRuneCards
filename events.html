Events This Month

Come and play. We run events every month for new and returning players. Never played before? You're exactly who our beginner-friendly events are for.

📍 Coronation Street, Blackpool

Usual weekly schedule: Disney Lorcana on Mondays, Magic: The Gathering on Tuesdays, Riftbound on Thursdays and Craft Night on Friday evenings.

Board Game Night runs every other Sunday: 8 and 22 November 2026, 6 and 20 December 2026, then every two weeks.

Dates can change, so check with us before you come. Month by month: November 2026 · December 2026

First time?

  1. Message us first on Discord or Facebook so we know you're coming.
  2. Tell us you're new when you arrive. We'll introduce you and pair you with a friendly player.
  3. No deck yet? Ask us before you come and we'll help you find the right starter.

What to bring

  • Your deck, ideally in sleeves.
  • Dice or counters if your game uses them.
  • The entry fee, if the event has one.
  • A free player account for official events. Our Learn to Play guide shows which one your game uses (step 5).

Bringing your child?

  • Playing together is one of the best ways to learn. Ask us which events suit families.
  • Under-16s should come with a parent or carer.
  • Want to get closer through play? Try our free 7 Days on the Same Side course.

Book your place

Message us to book, and to hear first about new sets, special nights and any changes to the calendar.
